Get your media karaoke-ready. Fast.

Search YouTube or paste a link to save videos, MP3s, and background images directly into your Local folders.
Ready for SongHub, your favorite karaoke app, or any background-media player.

Free to use. No ads. No subscriptions. Never for sale.

## 📲 Download

**[⬇️ Get the latest APK](../../releases/latest)** → open it on your phone and install `LyriaHub-x.x.x.apk`.
Android will ask you to allow installs from your browser or file manager the first time.
Once installed, LyriaHub tells you when a new version is out and can update itself from inside the app.

## 🇬🇧 English

### ✨ Key Features

- 🔎 **Search or paste** — search YouTube by name, or paste a video or playlist link. Searching remembers your last results.
- 🎬 **Video + audio, or video only** — saved as the original stream. Never resized, upscaled or re-encoded.
- 🎵 **MP3 or original audio** — MP3 at 128 / 192 / 320 kbps with title, artist and cover art, or the original M4A with no conversion.
- 🖼️ **Background images** — search free photos or pick one from your gallery. Every image is fitted to **1920 × 1080** (centered, never stretched).
- 🎤 **Karaoke-ready** — "Karaoke" and "Lyrics" search shortcuts, plus **key and tempo** control for karaoke videos.
- 📚 **Library** — favorites, playlists, search, sort, multi-select and video thumbnails. Share or open any file in another app.
- ⏬ **Smart downloads** — queue with pause / resume, Wi-Fi-only switch, survives phone restarts, clean file names, duplicate and low-storage warnings.
- 🧹 **Storage cleaner** — see what takes space and delete the biggest files.
- 🔒 **Private by design** — no ads, no third-party trackers. See the privacy notes in the app.
- 💛 **100% Free** — if it helps you, "Fuel the developer" in Settings is optional.

### 📁 Where your files go

LyriaHub saves into the `SongHub` folder on your phone. Existing files are never deleted or overwritten.

```text
SongHub/
├── videos/          # video + audio (.mp4 / .webm)
├── mp3s/            # MP3 and original audio (.mp3 / .m4a)
├── bgv/
│   └── Vids/        # silent background videos
└── bgphoto/         # background images (1920 x 1080 .jpg)
```

Android needs your OK for "All files access" the first time you download.

### ⚠️ Please note

Only download content you own or have permission to use. You are responsible for following YouTube's terms of service.

## 🇵🇭 Tagalog

### ✨ Mga Tampok na Feature

- 🔎 **Maghanap o mag-paste** — hanapin sa YouTube ang pamagat, o i-paste ang link ng video o playlist. Naaalala ng search ang huli mong resulta.
- 🎬 **Video + audio, o video lang** — sine-save bilang orihinal na stream. Hindi nire-resize, ina-upscale, o nire-re-encode.
- 🎵 **MP3 o orihinal na audio** — MP3 na 128 / 192 / 320 kbps na may title, artist at cover art, o ang orihinal na M4A na walang conversion.
- 🖼️ **Mga background na larawan** — maghanap ng libreng larawan o pumili sa gallery. Lahat ay ina-angkop sa **1920 × 1080** (nasa gitna, hindi iniuunat).
- 🎤 **Handa sa karaoke** — mga shortcut na "Karaoke" at "Lyrics" sa search, at kontrol sa **key at tempo** para sa karaoke video.
- 📚 **Library** — favorites, playlists, search, sort, multi-select at thumbnails. I-share o buksan ang file sa ibang app.
- ⏬ **Matalinong downloads** — queue na may pause / resume, Wi-Fi lang na switch, tuloy pa rin pagkatapos i-restart ang phone, malinis na pangalan ng file, babala sa duplicate at kulang na storage.
- 🧹 **Storage cleaner** — tingnan kung ano ang sumasakop ng espasyo at burahin ang pinakamalalaking file.
- 🔒 **Pribado** — walang ads, walang third-party tracker. Walang kinokolektang personal na data.
- 💛 **100% Libre** — kung nakatulong ito sa iyo, opsyonal ang "Fuel the developer" sa Settings.

### 📁 Saan napupunta ang mga file

Sine-save ng LyriaHub sa `SongHub` folder ng phone mo. Hindi binubura o ino-overwrite ang mga dati nang file.

```text
SongHub/
├── videos/          # video + audio (.mp4 / .webm)
├── mp3s/            # MP3 at orihinal na audio (.mp3 / .m4a)
├── bgv/
│   └── Vids/        # tahimik na background videos
└── bgphoto/         # background na larawan (1920 x 1080 .jpg)
```

Hihingin ng Android ang pahintulot na "All files access" sa una mong download.

### ⚠️ Paalala

Mag-download lamang ng content na pag-aari mo o may pahintulot kang gamitin. Ikaw ang responsable sa pagsunod sa terms of service ng YouTube.
